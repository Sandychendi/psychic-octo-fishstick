# Semiconductor Industry Research

## Project Overview

This project explores the global semiconductor supply chain, major industry participants, and the role of semiconductors in the global economy.

## Research Questions

1. What are the main stages of the semiconductor supply chain?
2. Which countries and regions play important roles?
3. What are the major opportunities and risks facing the industry?

## Research Method

- Use reliable and verifiable sources.
- Record the source and publication date of important claims.
- Distinguish facts from analysis and forecasts.
- Verify AI-generated information before using it.
