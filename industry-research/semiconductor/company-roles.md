# Key Companies in the Semiconductor Supply Chain

## 1. NVIDIA

**Primary role:** Fabless chip designer

NVIDIA designs GPUs and other computing products. Its business relies on a broader ecosystem of semiconductor foundries, packaging providers, memory suppliers, and other technology partners.

**Research question:** How does NVIDIA benefit from outsourcing wafer fabrication?

## 2. TSMC

**Primary role:** Semiconductor foundry

TSMC manufactures chips for customers using its semiconductor process technologies. Manufacturing performance depends on process capabilities, production scale, yield, and execution.

**Research question:** What makes advanced semiconductor manufacturing difficult to replicate?

## 3. ASML

**Primary role:** Semiconductor lithography equipment supplier

ASML supplies lithography systems used by semiconductor manufacturers. Its EUV technology is particularly important for producing many advanced logic chips.

**Research question:** Why is EUV lithography strategically important?

## 4. Initial Analysis

These companies operate at different stages of the semiconductor ecosystem.

NVIDIA focuses on chip design and computing products, TSMC specializes in wafer fabrication, and ASML supplies critical manufacturing equipment.

Their relationship illustrates how technological specialization creates interdependence across the global semiconductor supply chain.
